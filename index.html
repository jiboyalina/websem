Semantic Web Project / 01

Cycling,
connected.

A small exploration of how cycling knowledge can be described on the web: with clear concepts, shared meaning, and links between things.

The idea

Give cycling data a shared language.

An ontology gives information a structure that both people and software can understand. This project uses cycling as its subject, with room to describe riders, bicycles, and the routes that connect them.

01 / People

Riders

The people and roles involved in a ride.

02 / Equipment

Bicycles

The machines and parts used for cycling.

03 / Places

Routes

The paths, places, and journeys of a ride.
